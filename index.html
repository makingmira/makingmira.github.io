About the Jeweler

I'm an artistic generalist, melding years of experimenting into a practice that never quite finds a home in just one thing.

I've been making jewelry since I was five years old and metalworking for the past seven years. I continue to explore new beauty and forms in an ever-expanding creative process.

"Many pieces are one-of-a-kind, and make me as much as I make them."
